## 3. Socio-Ecological Context for Screenwriters

### A. The Climate Migrant Pipeline
* Daily, between 1,500 and 2,000 people arrive at Dhaka's Sadarghat launch terminal or train terminals, displaced by saline water intrusion into coastal paddy fields in Satkhira, Khulna, and Bagerhat.
* The Buriganga River and surrounding floodplains act as both economic lifelines and chemical conduits.

### B. Sensory Details for the Screen
* **The Soundscape:** Constant drone of low-rpm diesel generators, auto-rickshaw horns muffled by dense humidity, thunder rumbles vibrating through stagnant air, corrugated iron vibrating in sudden gusts.
* **The Visuals:** Grey sky blending seamlessly with brown river water; wet mud drying into grey powder along curbs; clothes sticking to backs; salt deposits on hand-pumped well faucets.
* **The Olfactory Palette:** Brackish canal mud, diesel fumes, pungent damp jute, wood fires, ozone before monsoon rain.
