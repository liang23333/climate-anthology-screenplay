# Anthology Story Bible & Narrative Architecture

## Core Vision
*Three elements. Three landscapes. One interconnected human struggle.*

This anthology weaves together three distinct narrative threads occurring against the backdrop of climate disruption:
1. **Rising Seas** (The Inundation) – Where land yields to the saltwater tide.
2. **Wildfires** (The Conflagration) – Where forests ignite and atmospheric smoke consumes civilization.
3. **Drought** (The Desiccation) – Where rivers empty and civilization fights over the final drops.

---

## Overarching Tonal Guidelines
* **Radical Realism:** Avoid cartoonish apocalyptic tropes. The horror of climate change is administrative, physiological, and generational. It is the slow failure of infrastructure, the quiet displacement of families, and the sudden, chaotic collision with planetary limits.
* **Sensory Immersion:** Ground every scene in tangible physical phenomena: the taste of salt in supposedly fresh drinking water, the gritty texture of ash falling like snow on a car windshield, the sound of an irrigation pump sucking dry gravel.
* **Global Interconnection:** The carbon emissions driving megafires in North America warm the oceans that drown the Bay of Bengal and bake the soil of the Amazon. Characters may never meet across segments, but visual, sound, and thematic motifs must link them.

---

## Recurring Motifs
1. **Water in Transformation:**
   - Segment 1: Excess / Intrusion (brackish floods, lost land, drowned roots).
   - Segment 2: Absence in the canopy / Fuel dryness (Vapor Pressure Deficit, pyrophytic clouds).
   - Segment 3: Scarcity / Evaporation (cracked riverbeds, ration tokens, dry wells).
2. **The Barometer / Atmospheric Gauges:**
   - In each segment, a character monitors an instrument: a tide marker in the delta, a heat sensor / air particulate monitor in the forest, a groundwater pressure meter in the valley.
3. **The Displaced Relic:**
   - An everyday object made obsolete or sacred by ecological collapse (a jar of heirloom seeds, an air filtration canister, a dry water deed).
